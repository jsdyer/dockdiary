WHAT THE DIARY RECORDS

Availability leaves a trace.

An empty station can mean high demand, maintenance, rebalancing or a paused service. These snapshots record available bikes and docks. They do not measure rides, trips or how long a station stayed empty between checks.

Sampling & coverage

The shared diary targets hourly captures for configured networks. GitHub schedules can be late or skipped. Direct lookups and imported diaries stay in this tab until you download them.

Heatmaps count distinct station timestamps, excluding missing counts, paused rental or return service for the selected measure, and readings older than the freshness limit at capture. A reported zero is preserved. Each cell uses the selected local time zone.

Source & privacy

Bike-share data supplied by CityBikes, through its public API. See its terms and attribution requirements. The public service permits 300 requests per hour. No API key is needed for ordinary use.

Opening this page reads saved data. A current lookup sends the selected network ID and your connection details to CityBikes. Station searches, heatmaps, imports and exports run in your browser. No location permission is requested.

For the rest of your route, explore Trailglass and Road Pulse.
